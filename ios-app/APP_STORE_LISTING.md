## Description
Amsterdam Guestlist Service is your key to the best nights in Amsterdam.

See what's on tonight, this week and next — from Jimmy Woo to Chin Chin Club — and get on the guestlist in one tap.

GET ON THE LIST
• Fill in your details once, then request any guestlist with a single tap
• Bringing friends? Add them to your request in seconds
• Get a notification and email the moment you're approved
• A reminder on the night, with the route and time
• Can't make it? Release your spot so someone else can go

NEVER MISS A NIGHT
• Every party in one place, grouped by tonight, this week and coming up
• Full guestlist? Join the waitlist and we'll ping you when a spot opens
• Discount tickets and table bookings for selected events
• Add any night to your calendar or share it to your story

EARN AS YOU GO
• Collect points for every night you're on the list
• Climb from Bronze to VIP and unlock more perks
• Invite friends and both of you earn

THE BACKROOM
• A members-only chat for every party you're approved for
• Private messages with other members — with block and report built in

AGS is for guests aged 18 and over. Some events have a higher minimum age.

> Amsterdam Guestlist Service (AGS) is a nightlife guestlist service in Amsterdam. Guests browse upcoming club nights and request a spot on the venue's guestlist; the AGS team approves the request and the guest receives a push notification and an email, plus a reminder on the night. Guests earn points for every night they are on the list.

>
> The Backroom (Account → Enter the Backroom) is a members-only chat.
