For venues & organisers

A full room, with the right crowd

AGS brings a steady, checked crowd to your night — and keeps them coming back. You decide the capacity, the door policy and who gets in.

  • Checked guestsFull names, age checks per event (including different limits for men and women) and a hand-picked approval for every request.
  • Fewer no-showsReminders on the night, a one-tap “can’t make it” that frees the spot for the waitlist, and a block after two no-shows.
  • Capacity you controlSet the number of spots and a deadline per night. When it’s full, guests join a waitlist instead of your DMs.
  • First look for our Inner CircleNew nights go to our private WhatsApp group and the app first — plus tickets and tables with your own links.
